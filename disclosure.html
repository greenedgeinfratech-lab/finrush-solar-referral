Finrush works for Greenedge under a written agreement. Under that agreement, Greenedge pays Finrush a service charge of 1% of the total business executed through this programme, and also pays Finrush the amounts needed for partner commissions and bonuses. Finrush then pays partners from its own account. The 1% service charge is not deducted from any partner's commission, and partners never pay anything to Finrush or Greenedge.

Customers buy their solar systems from Greenedge and pay Greenedge directly. Finrush does not collect money from customers.

Nature of the programme

The Finrush Solar Referral Programme is a referral-based commission programme tied strictly to the sale and installation of solar energy systems by Greenedge.

  • It is not a deposit, investment or collective investment scheme.
  • It is not a pyramid, chain or money-circulation scheme. There are no levels, no downlines and no income from recruiting.
  • There is no joining fee, mandatory purchase, inventory, target or rank purchase.
  • There is no fixed, guaranteed or passive income.
  • Income arises only from solar projects referred by the partner personally that are installed and fully paid.

Laws we follow

  • Consumer Protection Act, 2019, and Consumer Protection (Direct Selling) Rules, 2021
  • Prize Chits and Money Circulation Schemes (Banning) Act, 1978: the programme involves no money circulation
  • Income Tax Act, 1961: Finrush deducts TDS on commission under Section 194H
  • GST laws: all customer sales are invoiced by Greenedge
  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: see our Privacy Policy

Income disclaimer

Income depends solely on solar projects you refer that are installed and fully paid. No fixed, guaranteed or passive income is offered. Examples and calculator results on this website are illustrations, not promises. Earnings vary with effort, local demand, customer decisions and market conditions, and many partners may earn little or nothing.

Rules for partners' marketing

Partners must not make claims about subsidies, guaranteed savings, returns or fixed income, and must not present the programme as a job or investment. Any partner who does so may be terminated immediately. If a partner has made such a claim to you, please tell the Grievance Officer.

Grievance redressal

Partners, customers or members of the public can raise any complaint about this programme with Finrush. Complaints about a solar installation are passed to Greenedge and tracked until resolved.
