Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains how personal data submitted on the Finrush Solar Referral Programme pages at finrush.co.in is collected and used. It is written in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Who is responsible

Finrush Smart Services Private Limited owns this programme and decides how programme data is used (the "Data Fiduciary"). Referred customers' details are shared with Finrush's solar execution partner, Greenedge Infratech Private Limited, so it can survey, quote and install.

What we collect

  • Partner registration: name, mobile number, email (optional), city, district and state, occupation, and any message you add.
  • Customer referrals: your name, mobile and Partner ID; the customer's name, mobile number, city, project type, approximate size or bill, and notes.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and time of submission, used to prevent spam and abuse.
  • During verification (not on this website): PAN, Aadhaar and bank details, collected separately and only from registered partners, for KYC, TDS and payouts.

Why we use it

  • To register and verify partners and pay commissions, including TDS compliance.
  • To have Greenedge contact referred customers about solar systems.
  • To keep partners updated on referral status.
  • To meet legal, tax and audit obligations, and to handle complaints.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Customer data from referrals

Partners must have the customer's consent before submitting their details. If you were referred and do not want to be contacted, tell us on WhatsApp or by email and we will stop contacting you and delete your referral details, unless we must keep a record by law.

Sharing

Data is shared only between Finrush and Greenedge for running the programme, with service providers that help us (hosting, email, messaging), and with authorities where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Partner records and payout data are kept for as long as you are a partner, and then for up to 8 years as required by tax and accounting laws. Referrals that do not become customers are deleted within 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, withdraw consent, or raise a grievance by writing to finrushsmart@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 98370 67681. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

Submissions are sent over HTTPS and stored outside the public website folder with restricted access.
