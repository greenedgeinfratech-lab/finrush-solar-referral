How the bonus is counted

  • Confirmed sales = invoice value (excluding GST) of your referred projects that were installed and fully paid during that month.
  • Only the highest slab reached is paid. Slabs do not add up.
  • Sales by other partners never count towards your bonus. There is no team or downline income.
  • The bonus is paid on top of your direct commission, with the same timeline and TDS.
  • If a project is cancelled or refunded, the related commission and bonus are reversed.

FAQ

Common questions

Is there any joining fee?

No. Registration is completely free, and there are no renewal charges, kits or products you must buy.

Is this an MLM or investment scheme?

No. There are no levels, no team income and no investment. You earn only when a customer you personally referred installs a solar system and pays for it in full.

When is commission paid?

Within 15–30 working days after the project is installed, the customer has paid in full, and the completion certificate is issued. Finrush pays it to your registered bank account after TDS.

What if two people refer the same customer?

The first partner whose referral is recorded in our system, with the customer's correct mobile number, gets the credit. A referral stays valid for 90 days from submission.

Do I need solar knowledge?

No. You only make the introduction. Training is provided, and Greenedge's team handles survey, proposal and installation.

Is income guaranteed?

No. Income depends entirely on referrals that become installed and paid projects. Many partners may earn little or nothing.

What documents do I need?

After registration Finrush will ask for PAN (needed for TDS), Aadhaar for identity, and bank details for payouts. These are collected during verification by our team, not on the registration form.

How do I track my referrals?

Log in to your partner account with your mobile number and password. You'll see every lead's stage (contacted, survey, quotation, installed, paid), can add updates for our team, and can view your commission and payout history.

Who pays my commission, Finrush or Greenedge?

Finrush. This is a Finrush programme, and Finrush Smart Services Pvt. Ltd. pays all commissions and bonuses from its own account. Greenedge Infratech, Finrush's solar execution partner, sells and installs the systems and confirms to Finrush when a project is installed and paid.

Can I promise customers subsidy or savings?

Please don't. Subsidy depends on government rules and approval, and savings depend on usage. Share Greenedge's official proposal instead. False claims can lead to termination.
