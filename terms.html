These terms apply to everyone who registers as a Referral Partner ("Partner") in the Finrush Solar Referral Programme ("Programme"). The Programme is owned and run by Finrush Smart Services Private Limited (CIN U72900UP2021PTC145397; address 8/140, Raghuvir Puri, G.T. Road, Aligarh, Uttar Pradesh 202001) ("Finrush"). Solar systems referred under the Programme are sold, installed and serviced by Finrush's solar execution partner, Greenedge Infratech Private Limited, Aligarh ("Greenedge"), under a written agreement between the two companies. By registering, you agree to these terms with Finrush.

1. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old, resident in India, and legally able to enter into a contract. Businesses may register through an authorised person. Registration is subject to verification of identity (KYC), PAN and bank details by Finrush. Finrush may decline any application without giving reasons.

2. Nature of the relationship

You act as an independent referral associate. Nothing in the Programme makes you an employee, agent, franchisee or partner of Finrush or Greenedge. You may not sign contracts, accept payments, issue quotations or make commitments on behalf of either company.

3. Free registration

There is no joining fee, renewal fee, security deposit, starter kit or product you must buy. Neither company will ask you to pay any money to join, stay active, or qualify for commission. If anyone asks you for such a payment in the name of this Programme, please report it to the Grievance Officer.

4. Valid referrals

  1. A referral is a prospective customer, introduced by you with their consent, who is not already in contact with Greenedge.
  2. Referrals must be submitted through your partner account, your personal referral link, the official WhatsApp number, or another channel Finrush confirms in writing, with the customer's correct name and mobile number.
  3. If the same customer is referred by more than one person, the first valid referral recorded in Finrush's system gets the credit.
  4. A referral stays valid for 90 days from submission. If the customer has not placed a confirmed order within that period, the referral lapses, unless Finrush agrees in writing to extend it.
  5. Greenedge, as the seller and installer, decides acting reasonably whether to quote for or execute any project. Finrush decides whether a referral qualifies for commission.

7. When commission becomes payable

Commission and bonus become payable only after all of the following:

  • the customer has paid Greenedge the full invoice amount and the payment has been realised;
  • the system has been installed and commissioned; and
  • the completion certificate or handover document has been issued, and Greenedge has confirmed all of this to Finrush.

All commissions and bonuses are paid by Finrush from its own bank account, by transfer to your verified bank account, within 15–30 working days of the above. Where a customer pays in instalments or through a loan, commission is payable once the full amount is received.

8. Cancellations and refunds

If an order is cancelled, or Greenedge refunds the customer in full or in part, the related commission and bonus are not payable. If they were already paid, Finrush may recover them by adjusting future payouts or by asking you to repay them.

9. Tax

Finrush deducts TDS as per Section 194H of the Income Tax Act, 1961, where applicable, and issues TDS certificates. PAN is mandatory; without a valid PAN, TDS applies at the higher rate set by law. If you are GST-registered and required to charge GST on your commission, you must provide a valid tax invoice addressed to Finrush. You are responsible for your own income tax filing.

10. No recruitment income

You earn nothing for introducing other people to the Programme. There are no levels, downlines, team commissions, overrides or matching bonuses. Only projects referred by you personally qualify for commission or bonus.

11. No income guarantee

No fixed, minimum, guaranteed or passive income is offered. Examples on the website are illustrations only. Your earnings depend entirely on referrals that become installed and fully paid projects, and you may earn nothing.

12. Partner conduct

You must not:

  • make false or misleading claims about subsidy, price, savings, returns, warranties or government schemes;
  • describe the Programme as an investment, a job or a guaranteed-income opportunity;
  • collect any money from customers;
  • share a customer's details without their consent, or contact people who have asked not to be contacted;
  • use Finrush's or Greenedge's name or logo on your own materials without written approval;
  • offer bribes, kickbacks or improper inducements to anyone.

13. Suspension and termination

Either side may end participation at any time with written notice (email or WhatsApp is enough). Finrush may suspend or terminate a Partner immediately for breach of these terms, misrepresentation, fraud or unethical conduct. Commission already earned on valid referrals before termination will be paid, except where termination is for fraud or misrepresentation connected with that referral.

14. Your partner account

You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for activity under your account. Only the Finrush team can change a lead's stage or commission; partners can add leads and updates. Tell us immediately if you suspect misuse.

15. Personal data

Your data and the customer data you submit are handled as described in the Privacy Policy.

16. Changes to these terms

These terms may be updated with at least 30 days' notice on this page and by message to registered Partners. Changes do not affect commission already earned.

17. Grievances and disputes

Complaints can be raised with the Grievance Officer listed on the disclosure page. Complaints are acknowledged within 48 hours and resolved within 30 days. Disputes that cannot be settled amicably are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at Aligarh, Uttar Pradesh, and are governed by the laws of India.
